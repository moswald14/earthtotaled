# Dry Season

## Starting a farm

Find the allotment sign about 60 metres southwest of Shelter 09. Restoring a deer herd opens another waterworks site at its glade. Press **B** and scroll through the building kit. Place a cistern (3 iron) and pump (2 iron) within 12 metres. Both transformation paths have their own waterworks and clinics.

Use **E** on a prepared bed for the starter envelope. Cinderbean grows in about 65 seconds of watered world time and improves soil. Saltroot takes about 150 seconds and yields three rations. It grows faster in old lakebed soil or a bed enriched with mineral silt. Mendleaf takes about 115 seconds and yields two medicinal leaves. Each harvest returns seed. Dry plants go dormant and resume when watered. After two completely dry day/night cycles they wither, returning one seed to the farm stores. Open service hatches by exposed culverts for extra seed, silt and scrap.

Each connected cistern stores eight bottles and fills one every 90 seconds of world time. A field clinic takes one Mendleaf and two scrap per medpack, takes 75 seconds per order, and holds four completed or queued packs. A Bug can receive treatment directly at the clinic.

## Irrigation

With the Borer equipped, **R** cycles round, square and irrigation heads. The cistern's **Shape irrigation furrows** action also equips the right tool, including for a Bug. Stand clear of foundations. Tap fire to mark an inlet 4–6 metres from the tank. Aim 3–14 metres gently downhill and tap again. Further clicks continue from the last outlet. For a branch, stow and reopen the tool, then start at an existing outlet. End a flowing segment 3–4 metres from the centre of a bed. Near-level ground works. Rejected cuts explain which protection or slope obstructs them.

## Optional combat progression

Use **K** to see rank caps, next effects and costs. Learning a higher rank keeps your earlier ranks. The East Dispatch board and faction relay boards offer three repeatable night contracts. Accept a run, then select **Track run on map** for a waypoint. The third, fifth and tenth credited kills in an eight-second chain each earn a bonus. Quests, scavenging, farming and mining can also advance the survivor.

## Music and art

**Esc → Music** switches between the world score and Doodle Jazz, changes tracks and music volume, or mutes music. The three stereo recordings play offline. Music ducks for voices and combat. The original recordings use Doodle Jazz’s trio renderer with fixed arrangements in `Tools/render-dry-season.mjs`. To render them again, pass it a local Doodle Jazz checkout containing `jazz-core.mjs`. `Tools/make-dry-season.py` generates the editable farm and kit artwork for the Unreal content importer.
