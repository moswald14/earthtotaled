Make one EARTHtotaled schema 1 loot pack based on my idea. Ask what I want if I have not described it. Reply with a single plain JSON object. Use the example below as a shape and choose your own IDs and names.

{"schema":1,"id":"siltworks","name":"Siltworks Salvage","finds":[{"id":"cistern-seal","name":"Stamped cistern seal","source":"cache","chance":100,"use":"wrappers","yield":6},{"id":"optic","name":"Surveyor optic","source":"supertech","chance":60,"use":"scrap","yield":3}]}

Use 1 to 8 finds. IDs start with a lowercase letter and contain only lowercase letters, digits or hyphens (32 characters max). Names have 1 to 48 printable characters. Yield is an integer from 1 to 25. Supertech cannot supply water. Give every source and value in lowercase. Use exactly the fields in the example. Players redeem finds in the workshop.
